Our Story

Built for Curious Minds, for Over Decades

Named after Chief Hezekiah Oluwasanmi, former Vice-Chancellor of Obafemi Awolowo University, the library has grown from a modest campus reading room into one of Nigeria's leading academic libraries — home to vast print collections, digital archives, and quiet spaces built for focus.

Our Mission

Accessible knowledge for every student, researcher, and lifelong learner.

Our Vision

A trusted center for learning, innovation, and discovery.

Our Community

A welcoming space where curious minds explore ideas together.
